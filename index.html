About these tables

  • Names link to Wikipedia where an article exists, and otherwise to a search; titles link to Google Books or a search.
  • Pronunciations are given in IPA and are approximate for names with contested or regional readings.
  • Entries marked dates not established are living or lesser-documented authors whose birth years are not reliably recorded here.
  • Theoretical placements are interpretive summaries, not the authors' own self-descriptions.
